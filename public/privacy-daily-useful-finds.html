Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy describes how Daily Useful Finds ("we", "us" or "our") handles information in connection with our Instagram account @dailyusefulfinds00, our related automation, and this page. Contact us at [email protected].

Information we use

When you interact with our Instagram posts, Instagram may make information such as your username, comment text, comment and post identifiers, and timestamps available to us through its services and APIs. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and any information you include in your message.

Our site hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address and request logs when you visit this page. This page itself does not include analytics scripts or a contact form.

How we use information

We use information to operate our Instagram account, review interactions, respond to suitable comments, prevent duplicate automated replies, troubleshoot our services and respond to enquiries or deletion requests. Some replies may be generated with AI and posted automatically if that feature is enabled. Complex or sensitive comments are intended to be skipped by the current automated reply rules.

For the current organic-comment reply feature, comment text is classified locally in the workflow. Only a broad category, such as positive feedback or thanks, is sent to Google Gemini to generate a reply—not the original comment text, Instagram username or comment ID. The category is derived from a comment. Organic tip creation uses Gemini to help write captions without sending visitor comments for that purpose.

Service providers and public content

We use Meta/Instagram for our account and its API, GitHub for code hosting, automated workflows and this page, and Google Gemini for the AI functions described above. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and according to their own terms and policies. Providers may process information in countries other than yours.

Comments and replies posted on Instagram can be publicly visible according to Instagram's settings and rules. This policy does not control how Meta or other users use public content.

Storage and retention

Our automated reply workflow is designed not to print comment text or usernames in its normal logs. To help prevent duplicate replies, it may keep hashed comment identifiers, reply-text hashes, timestamps and post identifiers in a private GitHub repository. Hashes may still be linkable to an interaction. Workflow and service logs may also contain technical information.

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed for these purposes, to handle requests, maintain security and meet applicable obligations. Repository history, service logs and backups can persist after information is removed from a current file; their retention and removal depend on the relevant service and the steps needed to manage them. There is currently no fixed time-based deletion period configured for the reply-state file.

Request access, correction or deletion

To request deletion of information held by Daily Useful Finds, email [email protected] with the subject Data deletion request. Tell us your Instagram username and, if relevant, a link to the post or comment so that we can identify the information. Do not send your password, access token or identity documents.

We will review your request, may ask for reasonable information to verify it, and will take steps to locate and delete applicable information under our control, subject to legal and security obligations. We will consider relevant repository history and service logs as part of that process and explain any limitations to you.

You can also contact us at the same email to request access or correction, or to ask a privacy question. To delete content from Instagram itself or manage information held by Meta, use Instagram's own settings and privacy tools; contacting us does not automatically delete data held by Meta.

Changes

We may update this policy if our practices change. We will update the date at the top when we make changes.
